# Movie Reviews

A catalogue of **Movies** where **Members** post **Reviews**, so anyone can see how the community rates each **Movie**.

## Language

### Catalogue

**Movie**:
A film listed in the catalogue, maintained by an **Admin**.
_Avoid_: Film, title, show

**Certification**:
The audience age rating a **Movie** carries, such as PG-13 or R.
_Avoid_: Rating, age rating, MPA rating

**Genre**:
A broad category a **Movie** belongs to, such as Drama or Sci-Fi; a **Movie** can have several.
_Avoid_: Category, type

**Keyword**:
A narrower descriptive tag on a **Movie**, such as "Sword & Sandal" or "Time Travel".
_Avoid_: Tag, interest, label

**Trailer**:
A promotional video for a **Movie**, linked from a video site.
_Avoid_: Clip, preview, video

**Photo**:
A still image belonging to a **Movie**, apart from its poster.
_Avoid_: Image, gallery item, screenshot

### People

**Person**:
A real individual who worked on one or more **Movies**.
_Avoid_: Artist, celebrity, talent

**Credit**:
The link between a **Person** and a **Movie**, saying what they did on it: Director, Writer or Cast.
_Avoid_: Role, job, crew entry

**Cast Credit**:
A **Credit** for acting, which names the character played and its billing order; the first few are the **Movie**'s Stars.
_Avoid_: Actor, star, performer

### Where to watch

**Streaming Service**:
A platform where films can be watched, such as Netflix or Prime Video.
_Avoid_: Provider, platform, channel

**Streaming Link**:
A **Movie**'s listing on one **Streaming Service**, with the address to watch it there.
_Avoid_: Watch option, offer, availability

### Reviewing

**Review**:
A **Member**'s single verdict on one **Movie**: a required **Score** and an optional **Comment**. A **Member** has at most one **Review** per **Movie**.
_Avoid_: Rating (as a noun for the whole thing), post, feedback

**Score**:
The whole number from 1 to 10 inside a **Review**.
_Avoid_: Stars, points, rating

**Comment**:
The optional free text inside a **Review**.
_Avoid_: Message, review text, body

**Community Rating**:
The average **Score** across all **Reviews** of one **Movie**, shown with the number of **Reviews** behind it; absent until the first **Review**.
_Avoid_: IMDb rating, overall rating, average

### Library

**Watchlist**:
A **Member**'s private list of **Movies** they intend to watch.
_Avoid_: Wishlist, favourites, queue

**Watched**:
A **Member**'s private record that they have seen a **Movie**; posting a **Review** marks the **Movie** Watched.
_Avoid_: Seen, history, viewed

### Accounts

**Guest**:
Anyone browsing without signing in; can read **Movies**, **Reviews** and **Community Ratings**.
_Avoid_: Visitor, anonymous user

**Member**:
A signed-in person, shown publicly by their **Display Name**, who can keep a **Watchlist** and write, edit and delete their own **Reviews**.
_Avoid_: User, customer, reviewer

**Display Name**:
The public name a **Member** chooses, shown on their **Reviews** instead of their email.
_Avoid_: Username, nickname, handle

**Admin**:
A **Member** who can also add, edit and remove **Movies**, **People**, **Genres**, **Keywords** and **Streaming Services**.
_Avoid_: Moderator, staff
